College assignment · Art History

Five thousand years of Indian art,
in two interactive views.

This site presents Indian art history through two tools. The timeline follows fifteen landmark artifacts from the prehistoric rock shelters of Bhimbetka to the frescoes of Santiniketan. The art map places fifteen living regional traditions on the map of India, from Warli in Maharashtra to Pattachitra in Odisha.

Assignment 1

Interactive Timeline

Fifteen artifacts arranged across eleven era bands, from the Prehistoric period to the Contemporary. Scroll the timeline sideways, filter by era, and open any artifact for its full historical context. Use the arrow keys to move between pieces.

Open the timeline

Assignment 2

Interactive Art Map

Fifteen regional art forms pinned across India and colour-coded by category: folk and tribal, court and miniature, temple and devotional, and modern. Filter by category or search by name, state or artist.

Open the map

About the sources

Every image on this site comes from Wikimedia Commons and is either in the public domain or released under a Creative Commons licence. Each one is credited on the page where it appears, and all thirty are listed with their author, licence and source URL in CREDITS.md.

Where a date or an attribution is genuinely disputed by historians — the Didarganj Yakshi is the clearest case — the site says so rather than picking one answer. Those points are collected under “Verify before submitting” in README.md.
